# Machine_learning-capstone

### Problem Statement

Banks lose substantial revenue when customers close their accounts or stop using banking services.

Without predictive systems, banks often react to churn after it has already happened. This creates:

Revenue loss
Increased customer acquisition costs
Reduced customer lifetime value
Lower customer satisfaction

The objective of this project is to build a predictive system that can:
1. Predict whether a customer is likely to churn
2. Identify the major drivers of customer attrition
3. Help banks make proactive retention decisions
4. Provide data-driven insights through  visualizations

### Tools to use
Programming Language
Python
Python Libraries
Pandas
NumPy
Matplotlib
Seaborn
Scikit-learn

### Deployment
Streamlit

#### Insights to discover
* Whether customers with lower Credit Scores are more likely to churn
* How Geography affects customer retention and whether certain regions experience higher churn rates
* Whether Gender influences churn behavior
* The relationship between Age and customer attrition
* Whether customers with low Tenure are more likely to leave
* How Account Balance affects churn probability
* Whether customers with fewer banking products are at higher risk of churn
* The impact of owning a credit card  on customer loyalty
* Whether inactive customers churn more frequently
* How customer complaints relate to churn
* Whether customer Satisfaction Score influences retention
* The relationship between Card Type and churn behavior
* Whether loyalty or engagement levels (Point Earned) affect customer retention

  ### The project will help  businesses by,
  Helping them make data-driven decisions by uncovering the major factors influencing churn and use the insights to
* Improve customer service strategies
* Personalize marketing campaigns
* Design better loyalty programs
* Increase customer engagement
* Enhance customer satisfaction
